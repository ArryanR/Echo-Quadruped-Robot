<h3> Echo, its name inspired by the operating principle of ultrasonic distance sensors (which measure distance by reflecting sound waves ) is a custom-designed, open-source robotic quadruped. The project itself has a deep focus on spreading torque evenly, support systems, and kinematics (like inverse kinematics). This project, initially developed as a foundation for robotics, is now a passion project that I intend to carry on throughout my journey here. This robot was designed in Autodesk Fusion and Onshape.</h3>

<h3>When I build robots, I like them to have a purpose, and Echo is made to assist people with vision impairments to going to their destinations. It is designed to have an interface in which people with vision impairments can easily express their destination. Using computer vision and ultrasonic distance sensors, I’d like it to detect and move away from obstacles, leading the person in a safe way. To do this, I’m learning Computer Vision libraries, and am learning how to receive vision inputs, and turn them into physical outputs.
